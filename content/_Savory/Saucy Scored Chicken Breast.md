Adapted from [The Perfect Technique For Sauce Lovers](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/iEMB_p7DEF0).

## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- [ ] Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- [ ] 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- [ ] 1 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 2 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
- [ ] 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- [ ] 1 tbsp sherry vinegar
- [ ] 1 tbsp maple syrup
- [ ] 1 tbsp soy sauce

## Steps

1. If the chicken breasts are unevenly thick, gently pound only the thickest end until both breasts cook evenly. Do not flatten them completely.
2. Cut deep diagonal scores across the top of each breast, about 1 inch apart. The cuts should be deep but should not go all the way through the chicken. Season both sides generously with salt and pepper.
3. Lightly coat the chicken in flour and shake off the excess.
4. Heat the olive oil and 1 tablespoon of butter in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken until golden, about 3 minutes per side.
5. Lower the heat to medium. Add the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds, just until fragrant. Add the sherry vinegar, maple syrup, soy sauce, and remaining tablespoon of butter.
6. Tilt the skillet and repeatedly spoon the sauce over the chicken, especially into the scores, until the chicken reaches 165°F in the thickest part and the sauce is glossy, about 5 minutes. If the skillet gets dry before the chicken is cooked, add a splash of water.
7. Rest the chicken for 5 minutes. Slice and serve with the remaining pan sauce spooned over the top.

## Notes

- The deep scores are for sauce distribution, not tenderizing. They hold the butter–soy–maple sauce in each bite.
- Good with roasted potatoes and Brussels sprouts.
