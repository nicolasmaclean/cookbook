From the Quaker Oats box.

## Ingredients

Makes 4 dozen cookies.

- [ ] 1/2 cup + 6 tbsp butter, softened
- [ ] 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
- [ ] 1/2 cup sugar
- [ ] 2 eggs
- [ ] 1 teaspoon vanilla
- [ ] 1 1/2 cup flour
- [ ] 1 tsp baking soda
- [ ] 1 tsp cinnamon
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon salt
- [ ] 1 cup raisins
- [ ] 3 cups oats

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 350.
2. Beat butter and sugar.
3. Add flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt; mix well.
4. Add oats and raisins; mix well.
5. put dough onto ungreased cookie sheet (over ungreased baking pan)
6. Bake 8 to 10 minutes or until light golden brown. Cool for 1 minute then move to wire rack to finish cooling.

## Notes
